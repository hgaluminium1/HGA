Aluminium Ingots – Industries We Serve
HG Aluminium Smelters Limited manufactures 
aluminium ingots and secondary aluminium 
alloy ingots for downstream manufacturers, 
foundries, remelting units and metal-processing 
industries. Depending on alloy chemistry, grade 
and customer specification, our ingots can be 
used across casting, automotive, electrical, 
engineering and metallurgical applications.
Aluminium ingots are primarily used as a raw 
material for remelting, alloying and casting. The 
final end-use depends on the chemical 
composition and alloy grade of the ingot.
