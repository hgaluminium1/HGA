Aluminium Extrusion Profiles – Industries We 
Serve
HG Aluminium Smelters Limited manufactures 
aluminium extrusion profiles for a wide range of 
industrial, architectural and engineering 
applications. With the capability to produce 
customized sections in multiple alloy series, our 
extrusion profiles are designed to support 
demanding applications requiring strength, 
dimensional consistency, corrosion resistance 
and surface quality.
Our profiles can be supplied as per customer 
drawings and technical specifications for use 
across multiple downstream sectors.
